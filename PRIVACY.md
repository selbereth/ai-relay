# AI Relay privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

AI Relay is an Apple Watch app that sends questions to a server that you host and shows the answers. The developer does not operate any server for the app.

**What the developer collects:** nothing. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or accounts, and it sends no data to the developer or to any third party chosen by the developer.

**What the app stores on your watch:** the server address you typed (in the app's settings) and a pairing token (in the watch Keychain). Removing the app, or choosing "Re-pair this watch" or a different server address, deletes the token.

**What leaves your watch:** your questions, and a name for your watch (the device name), go to the server address you entered, over HTTPS (plain HTTP is allowed only for addresses on your own network). The server you chose then handles them. You control that server: what it stores, which AI service it forwards questions to, and how long history is kept. The AI Relay server can keep a history of questions and answers; you can switch that off or delete it on the server's Settings and Messages pages. Check the privacy terms of the AI service you connect to your server.

**Children:** the app has no accounts and collects no data. What the AI can answer depends on the service you connect.

**Changes:** updates will be posted at this address, with a new date.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/selbereth/ai-relay, or email the address on the App Store listing.
